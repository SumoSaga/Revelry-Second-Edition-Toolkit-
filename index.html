    Step 8

    Expertises

    You start with two Expertises plus additional Expertises equal to your Intellect. Write anything your character knows — a culture, a craft, a corner of the world. Name a weapon or piece of armor (try “Longsword”) and you gain that item's Gear Expertise as well.

    Step 9

    Experiences

    Create two vivid phrases. "Assassin of the Sapphire Syndicate" beats "Assassin."

    Each Experience adds its bonus to relevant rolls. Most are +2; some features (like the Automation heritage) grant a larger +3.
