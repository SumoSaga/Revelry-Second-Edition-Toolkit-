    Profile & Gear

    Adding one Element or Component costs an action. Willpower caps how many a single spell holds; your Rank in Arcana caps how many you can add in one turn. Casting the finished spell is a free action.
    Most of what an Element does is automatic and costs nothing — light, conditions, and the combinations that fire when the right parts meet. Six Elements also give the spell its own Strike, bought for one point of your margin in the exchange. Those appear in the maneuver menu on the Play Sheet, and your margin buys one of them, never all.
